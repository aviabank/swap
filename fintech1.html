4 Types of Interest Rate Swaps

Interest rate swaps come in different structures. AviaSwap supports all four — each fits a different aviation financing scenario.

01
📈

Fixed-to-Floating

Vanilla swap

Pay fixed rate, receive floating. Most common structure. Protects borrower against rising interest rates.

  • Airline with floating loan
  • Lock in monthly payment
  • Hedge against rate hikes
  • Notional in €B787, €A350 etc.
02
📉

Floating-to-Fixed

Reverse vanilla

Pay floating, receive fixed. Used when the counterparty has fixed-rate exposure but wants floating income.

  • Lessor with fixed income
  • Wants floating returns
  • Hedge against falling rates
  • Common in structured leases
03
🎯

Basis Swap

Two floating rates

Exchanges one floating rate for another (e.g. SOFR ↔ EURIBOR). Used to match currency of cashflows.

  • Multi-currency aviation deals
  • USD vs EUR exposure
  • Cross-currency hedge
  • Reduces FX mismatch risk
04
🔀

Amortizing Swap

Declining notional

Notional amount declines over time, matching the depreciation schedule of the aircraft. Most realistic hedge for aircraft loans.

  • Notional tracks aircraft value
  • Matches loan amortization
  • Lower premium over time
  • Ideal for aircraft financing
